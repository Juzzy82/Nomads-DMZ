Hajin Bunker Loot Route in MW4 DMZ: All Six Bunkers in Order

There are six bunker markers along the west side of Hajin. This route links them in one north-to-south run, so you can search each one and decide at every stop whether to carry on or extract.

  1. Pick your end of the route

    Open the numbered map before you deploy and start at the end nearest your deployment. Stop 1 is in the north, above the 14th Political Prison. Stop 6 is the southernmost, on the western edge below Deadtown.

  2. Run it in order

    Travel to the next numbered marker, search the bunker, then decide whether to continue or extract. Stop 4 sits just west of the Chang-san Air Base, so expect company there.

  3. Check the map between stops

    The numbered map shows where the bunkers are. It does not show a safe path, active threats or extraction points, so check your tactical map before every move.

  4. Protect what you find

    Extract early when the risk of another stop outweighs what you might find there.

Tip: The route can be run in reverse. Start in the south if that is closer to your deployment.
