How to Open Irradiated Items in DMZ (Haneul Nuclear Reactor Guide)

Found an irradiated item in DMZ? Keep it. You can take it to the Haneul Nuclear Reactor and process it for a chance at some gear.

  1. Before you go in

    Bring gas masks if you have them. The reactor is a radiation zone, so go in prepared. If you haven't got a mask, the bots around the facility can drop them. A hazmat suit can also spawn inside.

  2. Finding the way in

    Head to the second reactor on the right and look for the broken staircase at the back. Climb up to the upper level, take the ladder, then jump across the gap to the next section.

  3. Getting to the bottom

    Once you're across, climb the next ladder and work your way down through the reactor to the ground floor. Watch your footing on the way down. If you've got a parachute, you may be able to use it for a quicker descent.

  4. Processing your item

    Look for the processing machine in the corner on the ground floor. Walk up with your irradiated item and follow the prompt to put it in. Give it a few seconds to finish, then collect your loot.

Tip: Keep an eye out for spare gas masks and the hazmat suit while you're in there. They'll make the run easier.
