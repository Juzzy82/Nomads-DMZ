Gorani boss strategy in MW4 DMZ

Lieutenant. Camouflage, rifle fire, decoys, smoke and escort ambushes.

Location: Forest south of Cheonwang Peak Array. Recommended kit: Scoped assault rifle, full armour, spare ammunition. Possible rewards: Dog Tag Case; possible MAR-9 / Incisor variant..

  1. Approach the woodland camp from cover and watch for camouflaged escorts.
  2. Clear the guards before committing to Gorani himself.
  3. Look for decoy targets and avoid emptying entire magazines into false targets.
  4. Reposition around smoke without relying on it to stop incoming fire.
  5. Identify the real Gorani, close the distance through cover and finish with sustained fire.
  6. Check the surroundings before retrieving his dog tag.

Top tip: Don’t chase every figure in the smoke. Confirm the real target and use cover to close in.

The exact active encounter area can vary; confirm using intel or a Hunt Tower.
