      Home›X01 Facility
      X01 FacilityUnderground facility · Season 1

      Getting in

      Enter X01 through the abandoned elevator shaft by the facility in north-west Hajin. It is the X01 marker on the Hajin map.

      Watch the clock

      A toxic gas timer starts when you arrive (about 20 minutes). Players report the facility then shuts down sector by sector over about five minutes, and normal exfils close first.

      The final exfil lift in Production Assembly reportedly takes about 30 seconds to call and 25 seconds to hold, so start it early.

      Attachment slots

      DMZ rules: Gunsmith unlocks at DMZ Level 10. Extract a Weapon Manual to unlock buying that base weapon (it also pays 5,000 DMZ Cash). Base weapons start with no attachments: buy up to five attachment slots, each costing more than the last, plus the attachments themselves. The Apex slot opens after leveling the weapon. Looted Hajin weapons can carry up to eight. Fail to exfil and the weapon is lost.

      Base M4 is about 5,000 DMZ Cash. Blueprints are free to use once and lost for good if lost in a match (rebuy at a discount). Camos, stickers and charms cost nothing.

      About this pageTae-Hyun boss strategy in MW4 DMZ

      Lieutenant. A new Season 1 Lieutenant; Boss Board intel gives more detail before you meet him.

      Location: X01 lower floor, around the Data Center. Recommended kit: Automatic rifle, full armour, spare plates, Self-Revive. Possible rewards: $10,000 the first time (Boss Board), plus $25,000 for each of their Dog Tags you extract. Your first ever Lieutenant takedown adds a one-off $10,000. Item drops not confirmed yet..

      1. Buy his intel on the Boss Board before deploying.
      2. Clear guards around his position before engaging him.
      3. Fight from cover and avoid long open corridors.
      4. Use short peeks and stagger squad fire and reloads.
      5. Replate behind solid cover.
      6. Loot his Dog Tag and extract with it for the bonus payout.

      Top tip: X01 closes its exfils as time runs down. Plan your route out before starting the fight.

      How he fights hasn't been published yet. This guide will be updated once it's confirmed. X01 boss spots are player-reported and not yet confirmed in game.
