MW4 DMZ operator traits planner

Plan an 18-point trait build across the Warfighter, Scout and Survivalist trees. Each row takes one pick, and minor and opening traits are randomised.

Warfighter trait tree

Major traits

  • Equipment Professional: Slowly recharges equipped Lethals and Tacticals after use. Refills up to the max capacity of the slot.
  • Combat Resistance: Reduces damage from explosives and the effects of enemy Tacticals.
  • Viper Sense: Conceals you from thermal optics and AI targeting systems.
  • Quick Fix: Reduces health regeneration delay when an enemy operator or hostile is killed.
  • Possum: Allows a Self Revive into Last Stand from death, restoring health to maximum, once per match. You cannot activate it while the team that killed you is nearby, or until 4 minutes have passed.
  • Adaptive Restock: Enemy hostiles drop one additional item.

Scout trait tree

Major traits

  • Hostile Looter: Enemy hostiles sometimes drop an Uncommon printer ingredient, with a very small chance for a Rare printer ingredient.
  • Container Looter: Chance to get an extra piece of loot in containers.
  • Thermal Padding: Hides you from UAVs and downgrades Advanced UAVs to UAV detection.
  • UAV Upgrade: Increases the range and duration of UAVs, and upgrades them to Advanced UAVs.
  • Overwatch: UAV towers you activate never expire.
  • Field Crafting: Allows crafting items directly out of Common printer resources while deployed.

Survivalist trait tree

Major traits

  • Faster Revive: Increases revive speeds.
  • Consumable Duration: Increases the duration of consumables.
  • Impact Smokes: Releases smoke when downed.
  • Free Revive: Automatically repairs your first Wound.
  • Numb the Pain: Provides Painkiller effect on revive.
  • Die Hard: Approaching an exfil while in Last Stand calls the exfil automatically. Bleed-out time in Last Stand is unlimited.

Prestige traits

  • Adaptive Restock Pro (Warfighter): Enemy hostiles drop rarer additional items.
  • Possum Pro (Warfighter): Allows a Self Revive into Last Stand from death, and removes the tourniquet health penalty, once per match.
  • Quick Fix Pro (Warfighter): Immediately begin health regeneration when an enemy operator or hostile is killed.
  • Viper Sense Pro (Warfighter): Conceals you from thermal optics and AI targeting systems. Also alerts you when an enemy Operator is aiming at you.
  • Overwatch Pro (Scout): UAV towers you activate never expire. Also increases their range.
  • UAV Upgrade Pro (Scout): Increases the range and duration of UAVs and upgrades them to Advanced UAVs. Also expands your minimap.
  • Thermal Padding Pro (Scout): Enhances Thermal Padding to hide occupied vehicles from UAV detection.
  • Field Crafting Pro (Scout): Allows crafting items directly out of Common printer resources while deployed, and reduces the resource cost of each craft by 50%.
  • Free Revive Pro (Survivalist): Automatically repairs your first Wound. Also allows you to tourniquet while Wounded.
  • Numb the Pain Pro (Survivalist): Upgraded version of Numb the Pain. Its exact effect is not confirmed yet.
  • Impact Smokes Pro (Survivalist): Increases size and duration of smoke released for Impact Smokes.
  • Die Hard Pro (Survivalist): Approaching an exfil while in Last Stand calls the exfil automatically. Also grants infinite bleed out time and faster movement speed while in Last Stand.

Minor traits (available in every tree)

  • Adrenaline Burst: Killing an enemy resets tactical sprint.
  • Air Superiority: Extends the duration of UAV towers and SAM sites.
  • Ammo Reserves: Increases the ammo reserve amount for all ammo types.
  • Ammo Scavenger: Gain double ammo from ammo packs.
  • Bounty Hunter: Increases rewards for collecting bounties.
  • Bounty Intel: Decreases the cost of purchasing Bounty Intel.
  • Combat Scanner: Downing an enemy Operator sends out a UAV pulse at their position. Not yet confirmed in game.
  • Combat Scavenger: Hostiles have a small chance to drop an ingredient or valuable when killed.
  • Consumable Stacker: Using a consumable that is already active stacks the durations together, up to twice the base duration.
  • Expedited Extraction: Extractions arrive much faster.
  • Field Medic: Revives allies faster.
  • Final Intel: Final exfil marked on the Tac Map from the start.
  • Fuel Economy: Lowers vehicle fuel consumption.
  • Hostile Ammo: Hostiles drop an additional ammo pack when killed.
  • Investor: Slightly increases cash amount extracted.
  • Key Cash: Opening a locked space with a key grants cash. Cash increases by rarity of the key.
  • Killing Spree: Killing enemies increases the duration of active effects, up to twice their normal duration.
  • LT Hunter: Activating a Lieutenant Tower reveals an additional Lieutenant.
  • Lucky: When suffering from a negative effect or short on supplies, increases the chances of finding a useful item.
  • Mechanic: Doubles how fast you refuel and repair vehicles. Not yet confirmed in game.
  • Metabolism: Slowly regenerates the health you lose to the Tourniquet.
  • Mini Tracker: Looting an unopened container has a 50% chance to emit a radar pulse.
  • Objective Oriented: Op reward crates are one rarity tier higher, up to Rare.
  • Pack Rat: Raises your maximum 3D Printer resource capacity by 20%. Stacks with Resource Hoarder.
  • Plate Organization: Lets you carry two more reserve Plates. Stacks with Plate Pouch. Not yet confirmed in game.
  • Plate Pouch: Increases reserve Plate storage by 2. Stacks with Plate Organization.
  • Plate Speed: Decreases the time it takes to insert a plate.
  • Popcorn Lethals: Hostiles hit by your Lethals have a higher chance to drop Lethals.
  • Popcorn Tacticals: Hostiles affected by Tacticals have an increased chance to drop Tacticals.
  • Quick Knife: Replaces weapon bash melee attacks with knife attacks.
  • Rad Blocker: Lowers damage taken from radiation.
  • Rampage: Applies a short Painkiller effect when you kill an enemy Operator. Not yet confirmed in game.
  • Rescuer: Move faster while dragging a downed teammate.
  • Resource Hoarder: Raises how many 3D Printer resources you can carry. Stacks with Pack Rat. The exact amount is not published yet.
  • Resource Management: Printer resources picked up over your cap go to your lowest resource instead.
  • Resource Pockets: Scrapped items grant 20% more printer resources.
  • Resupply: Ammo restocks and ammo crates give extra ammo. Armor boxes give 2 extra armor plates.
  • Strong Arm: Lets you throw equipment farther and shows a trajectory line for your throws. Not yet confirmed in game.
  • Supplied Drops: Chance for a supply drop to include an extra container. Applies to entire squad and stacks.
