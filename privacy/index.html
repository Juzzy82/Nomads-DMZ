Privacy

NOMADS DMZ HUB is an unofficial fan site and is not affiliated with Activision. Logging in is optional. Without it, your progress stays in your own browser.

What we store when you log in

  • Your Discord number, your Discord name and your Discord picture. We get these from Discord when you log in, and we refresh them each time.
  • When your account was made and when you last logged in.
  • The progress you save in the Upgrades tracker and the Camo Tracker.
  • The private pins you add to the map: where each one is, and its name, note, colour and icon. Only you can see them.
We do not store your email, your server list, your messages or your Discord access token. The site only asks Discord who you are, uses the answer once, and hands the token back to Discord straight away.

Cookies

One cookie keeps you logged in for up to 30 days. It is needed for logging in to work. We use no advertising or tracking cookies.

Who else handles your data

  • Discord checks who you are when you log in.
  • Cloudflare hosts the site and the database that holds the information above.

Your choices

  • Download your data: open the account menu on the home page and choose Download my data.
  • Delete your account: choose Delete my account in the same menu. Your account, saved progress, pins and logins are removed straight away.
  • Log out any time. You can also choose to remove the progress saved in that browser.
