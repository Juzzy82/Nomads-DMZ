MW4 DMZ Dynamic Ops guide

Short side operations you start from the Tac Map or a live field phone.

Viral Reckoning

Scientist rescue / WMD sabotage. How to start: Dynamic Operations deploy mode or activate a field phone. Panel cash example $7,500. HVT-radio opening and UAV opening; continuation after scientist rescue.

  1. Get scientist intel: marked HVT and radio, or assigned UAV-intel interaction.
  2. Reach the scientist and unlock the room using the current objective code.
  3. Carry scientist to the landing zone; defend until the helicopter arrives.
  4. Put the scientist in the helicopter.
  5. Choose the offered partial-credit exfil or continue to collect the explosive.
  6. Plant the explosive on the assigned biological WMD and confirm completion.

Ghost Shipment

HVT / SAM interception. How to start: Dynamic Operations deploy mode or activate a field phone. Panel cash example $7,500. Cargo recovery can follow aircraft destruction.

  1. Eliminate the marked HVT.
  2. Pick up Classified Orders; the kill alone is not the intel pickup.
  3. Approach the assigned SAM site; capture and defend it.
  4. Wait for the aircraft objective to resolve.
  5. Loot the marked Lieutenant cargo container when the tracker requests it.

Chemical Denial

Chemical crafting OR server defence. How to start: Dynamic Operations deploy mode or activate a field phone. Panel cash example $5,000. The chemical-crafting and power/hack/defend routes are alternatives.

  1. Craft route: retrieve the marked item, as Classified Documents.
  2. Collect three assigned chemical mission components.
  3. Use Craft Compound at the marked workbench.
  4. Deposit the Unstable Compound in the assigned case; move clear and confirm completion.
  5. Alternative server route: turn on power, hack the Comms Server, protect the transfer, then confirm completion.

Toxic Intercept

Decryption / fuel / vehicle delivery. How to start: Dynamic Operations deploy mode or activate a field phone. A drive-to-tanker route exists; other versions mention bomb defence.

  1. Recover the encrypted drive from the assigned cache.
  2. Decrypt at the marked enemy computer.
  3. Defend the transfer until the tracker advances.
  4. Use Request a Fuel Resupply at the assigned UAV tower.
  5. Hijack the marked tanker.
  6. Deliver it to the assigned destination; confirm the required interaction and completion.

Dead Man's Cache

Intel / cache demolition / HVT. How to start: Dynamic Operations deploy mode or activate a field phone. Panel cash example $7,500. Intel openings and bomb containers vary.

  1. Complete the assigned intel opening: cache, encrypted-drive or enemy-comms variant.
  2. Complete the keypad or terminal stage if assigned; use current code.
  3. Download the target cache location.
  4. Collect the marked mission bomb.
  5. Destroy the assigned ammunition/armour cache.
  6. Continue to the HVT objective when shown; eliminate it and confirm completion.

Signal Breaker

Bomb crafting / multiple sabotage targets. How to start: Dynamic Operations deploy mode or activate a field phone. Panel cash example $10,000. Part containers and target assignments vary.

  1. Collect all three assigned mission bomb parts.
  2. Craft the bomb at the marked field workbench.
  3. Plant at each of three assigned radar-truck targets.
  4. Confirm operation completion after the final target.

Broken Resupply

Hack / safe intel / WMD sabotage. How to start: Dynamic Operations deploy mode or activate a field phone. Panel cash example $5,000. Bomb containers and keypad codes differ.

  1. Hack the assigned Comms Server.
  2. Defend until that stage completes.
  3. Retrieve the marked bomb from the assigned container.
  4. Open the assigned safe to obtain keypad intel.
  5. Unlock the marked room using the current code.
  6. Plant on the WMD; move clear and confirm completion.

Special Delivery

Bomb crafting / launcher destruction. How to start: Dynamic Operations deploy mode or activate a field phone. Panel cash example $7,500. Laptop upload and UAV capture alternatives.

  1. Collect three bomb components: Explosives, Explosives Wiring, Explosives Numpad in the documented route.
  2. Craft the bomb at the field workbench.
  3. Complete the assigned laptop upload OR UAV capture stage.
  4. Reach the marked Mobile Launch Platform.
  5. Plant the bomb and complete the defence.
  6. Take cover and wait for detonation/completion.

Domino Protocol

Linked HVTs / Lieutenant hunt. How to start: Dynamic Operations deploy mode or activate a field phone. Assigned targets and the final Lieutenant vary.

  1. Eliminate first HVT and collect Classified Documents.
  2. Eliminate second HVT and collect its intel.
  3. Follow the resulting Lieutenant objective.
  4. Defeat the assigned Lieutenant; confirm operation completion.

Sky Heist

Intel / aircraft theft. How to start: Dynamic Operations deploy mode or activate a field phone. Intel openings vary; a separate helicopter-hacking alternative .

  1. Complete the assigned intel opening: safe/cache/locker or power-terminal route.
  2. Complete a SAM stage if the tracker assigns one.
  3. Eliminate the marked pilot and collect the helicopter mission key.
  4. Unlock the assigned heavy helicopter.
  5. Follow the live timed flight/exfil objective.
  6. Read the leave/extract options and current completion state before deciding whether to stay aboard.

Hunt Operator

Target hunt. How to start: Dynamic Operations deploy mode or activate a field phone. Named wanted target versus rival-squad hunt.

  1. Read the target identity and objective on the current tracker.
  2. Eliminate the assigned target or squad as specified.
  3. Confirm completion before treating the hunt as finished.

Ignition Point

Reach target area. How to start: Dynamic Operations deploy mode or activate a field phone. Panel cash example $5,000. Only a short reach-area route exists.

  1. Proceed to the marked target area.
  2. Confirm the operation-completion message; follow any additional objectives actually shown.
